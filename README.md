# Certified Low-Degree Coefficient-Minor Positivity for Fibonacci Matching Transforms

Research No.61 · Preprint v1.0 · 2026-11-10

Ryutaro Yonezu (Independent Researcher)

This computer-assisted proof establishes strict positivity of the adjacent 2-by-2 coefficient minors of two auxiliary matching-polynomial products for integers satisfying

$$
3 \le k \le 180,\qquad n \ge 2k+4,\qquad 0 \le r \le k.
$$

An exact Newton-basis certificate handles every integer n in that range for each certified k. A second construction certifies the original minors directly for k=3..22. The all-k statement is unproved. The original Elizalde, Rivera Jr., and Zhuang Conjecture 4.6 remains unsolved.

- **Independent AI mathematical and editorial audits** reported PASS within the scope reviewed.
- **Windows reproduction** passed on the supplied real-machine run. All 12 ranges covering k=3..180 were regenerated, and the original archive checksum and full runner passed. Of 8,053,165 Newton coefficients, 8,036,344 were positive, 16,821 were zero, and none were negative.
- **Novelty** is INCONCLUSIVE. Human peer review was not performed.
